Accessibility Statement

Last updated: October 23, 2026

At Skilled Services Plus (ss+), we believe everyone should be able to access information and services online. We are committed to improving the accessibility and usability of our website for all visitors, including people with disabilities.

Our approach

We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA by working toward clear navigation, readable content, sufficient color contrast, keyboard accessibility, and compatibility with assistive technologies.

Ongoing improvements

Our website has not yet undergone a complete accessibility evaluation. Some features may not work equally well for all visitors. We will continue reviewing and improving accessibility as the website develops.

Feedback and assistance

If you experience difficulty accessing our website or need information in an alternative format, please contact us. We welcome your feedback and will do our best to assist.
