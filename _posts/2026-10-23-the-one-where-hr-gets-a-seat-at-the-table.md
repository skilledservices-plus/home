For much of its history, Human Resources focused on administration, employee records, compliance, and personnel matters. That began to change in the 1990s when Dave Ulrich's work helped reposition HR as a strategic business partner. His Three-Legged Stool Model organized HR into **three interconnected functions**: ***HR Business Partners (HRBPs)*** advising business leaders, ***Centers of Excellence (COEs)*** providing specialized expertise, and ***HR Shared Services (HRSS)*** managing routine operations.

At its core, the model calls on HR to act as a **Strategic Partner, Change Agent, Employee Champion, and Administrative Expert**, connecting workforce decisions to business outcomes. This thinking also shaped the evolution of Skilled Services Plus (ss+), from its roots in HR to a broader approach to professional services and strategic partnership. It reinforced a principle we believe in: *expertise should go beyond completing tasks to helping businesses make better decisions and achieve meaningful results*. **The Plus Side? *When we understand how people, processes, and business performance work together, we're better equipped to harness people, strengthen processes, and scale profits.***
